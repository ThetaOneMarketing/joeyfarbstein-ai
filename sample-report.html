4 · How you work with it

Your habits

Most of your chats are one question, one answer, done. 140 of 212 are a single message. You fire it off, grab the answer, and leave. That works for a quick text. It falls apart for a quote, where the first answer is never the one you send.

You use it at night. 9 to 11 pm is your busiest window, Tuesdays and Sundays most. That's the office work piling up after the crew goes home.

You trust it with words. You don't bring it numbers, your calendar, or who owes you money. None of that shows up in four months of chats.

You use AI like a smart notepad for customer words. One question at a time, late at night, and it has no idea who you are.

6 · Where it let you down

Three patterns

Each one has two examples from your chats and a fix.

It keeps forgetting you

38 chats start by telling it what you do and where. The answers come back generic because it only knows what you typed that minute.

  • "I'm a roofer in Spokane, write a text to..." 14 times in September
  • Three quotes in one week each re-explained your pricing
Fix

Paste a short "who I am" block at the top of every chat. It's in step 7.

You give up after one bad answer

When the first answer misses, you leave. 31 chats end after one reply that clearly wasn't what you wanted.

  • Warranty letter, July 9. One reply, never finished.
  • Lien notice, August 22. Same.
Fix

Tell it what was wrong in one line. The second answer is usually the one.

Same text, written fresh every time

The after-job follow-up text got written 23 separate times. They're all about the same.

  • "Text to say thanks and ask for a review", 23 times
  • "Text that we're coming tomorrow", 9 times
Fix

Save the best one. Paste it, change the name, send.

All three of these are rung 1 problems. They happen because the tool doesn't know you yet, and the first fix in step 7 clears most of them.

7 · Three things to do tonight

Three small changes, free, about ten minutes each.

All of them work in the app you already use.

Your follow-up textWhy: You wrote this 23 times.

Save this in your phone. Change the name. Send it the morning after every job.

Instead of a new chat every time, keep one going per customer or per job. It'll remember what you told it last week.
