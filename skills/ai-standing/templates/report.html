1You type, it answers{{RUNG1_YOU}}

Every chat starts from zero. It doesn't know you or your customers, so you re-explain each time, and you still do the real work yourself.

What you get back: It types faster than you do. The thinking and the fixing are still yours.

2It knows your business{{RUNG2_YOU}}

Your prices, your area, and how you talk to customers are already in it. You stop re-explaining and the answers come back sounding like you.

What you get back: Texts and quotes come out close to right the first time, so most of the rewriting goes away.

3It's hooked up to your tools{{RUNG3_YOU}}

It can see your calendar, your invoices, and your texts, so it works from what's going on instead of what you remember to tell it.

What you get back: You can ask who hasn't paid or what's on Thursday and get a real answer without looking it up.

4It runs jobs without you{{RUNG4_YOU}}

Follow-ups, quote drafts, and reminders go out on their own. You check in on them instead of doing them.

What you get back: Hours a week back, because the office work that piles up at 9 pm mostly stops piling up.
